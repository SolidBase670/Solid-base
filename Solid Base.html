About this website
Solid Base turns your idea into something real: a website, a mobile app or a clean design. Here is what we can do for you, and how this website works, in plain words.


    
What we can do for you

    

      
Websites and web apps
Business websites, online shops, booking pages, school or church portals and dashboards. Tell us what you want to achieve, and we build it.

      
Mobile apps
Apps for iPhone and Android, built once with Flutter. Good for businesses, services, delivery, learning and community ideas.

      
UI/UX design
Wireframes and screens designed in Figma before any code is written, so you can see and approve how it will look and feel.

      
Assignment and project help
Stuck on a school, university or final-year project? We guide you step by step, explain the code and help you build and test it, so you understand your own work. Please follow your school's rules.

      
Fix or upgrade what you have
Slow site, broken page, old design? We repair it, make it faster and mobile friendly, or add new features like login, forms and payments.

      
Launch and support
We help you choose a domain, put your site online, connect WhatsApp and contact forms, and stay available for updates after launch.

Common questions

    
Does it cost anything to create an account?
No. Creating an account costs nothing. You only discuss prices when you ask us to build something.

    
How long until I get a reply?
We reply as soon as we can. Your message card updates live, so you can see when we have seen it and when we have answered.

    
Can I download the code of your projects?
Yes, for projects that show a Download demo code button. They are demos you can study and learn from.

    
Do you really help with assignments?
Yes. We guide and teach you, explain the code and help you build and test, so you learn and can explain your own work. Always follow your school's or lecturer's rules.

    
How do we agree the price?
After your message, we discuss what you need, the time and the cost. Nothing starts until you say yes.

    
Something went wrong. What do I do?
Go to Contact and choose Complaint. We read every message, and you can watch the status until it is answered.


    
Not sure where to start?
Tell us your idea in a few lines. We will suggest the best option.
